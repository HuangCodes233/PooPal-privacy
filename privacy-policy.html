概述

PooPal（"便便伙伴"）是一款个人生活习惯记录工具与趣味养成应用。我们非常重视您的隐私，本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

我们收集的数据

  • 排便时间记录
  • 便便类型（Bristol 分类，可选）
  • 应用设置偏好（主题、通知时间等）

以上数据均仅存储在您的设备本地（或您主动开启的 iCloud 账户中），我们的服务器不会收集、存储或传输您的任何个人数据。

HealthKit 数据

如果您授权本应用访问 Apple 健康数据，相关数据仅在您的设备本地用于趋势展示，不会上传至任何服务器或与第三方共享。您可以随时在系统设置中撤销授权。

第三方服务

本应用不包含任何第三方分析工具、广告 SDK 或追踪器。您的数据不会被分享给任何第三方。

数据存储与删除

所有记录数据存储在您的设备本地。您可以随时：

  • 在历史记录中左滑删除单条记录
  • 删除应用以清除全部数据

我们不保留您的任何数据副本。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

健康免责声明

本应用仅为个人习惯记录工具，不构成任何形式的医疗诊断、健康评估或治疗建议。应用内展示的数据趋势和统计信息仅供参考。如有健康问题，请咨询专业医生。

隐私政策变更

如本政策有更新，我们会在应用内通知您。继续使用本应用即表示您同意更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy · Last updated: March 18, 2026

Overview

PooPal is a personal habit tracking tool and fun virtual pet app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

  • Bowel movement timestamps
  • Stool type (Bristol Scale classification, optional)
  • App preferences (theme, notification settings, etc.)

All data is stored locally on your device (or in your iCloud account if you enable it). Our servers do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

HealthKit Data

If you authorize this app to access Apple Health data, it is used only locally on your device for trend display purposes. It is never uploaded to any server or shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in your device settings.

Third-Party Services

This app does not contain any third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or trackers. Your data is never shared with any third party.

Data Storage & Deletion

All records are stored locally on your device. You can at any time:

  • Swipe to delete individual records in the history view
  • Delete the app to remove all data

We do not retain any copies of your data.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health Disclaimer

This app is a personal habit tracking tool only and does not constitute any form of medical diagnosis, health assessment, or treatment advice. Data trends and statistics displayed in the app are for reference only. Please consult a healthcare professional for any health concerns.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, we will notify you within the app. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
