隐私政策 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 4 日

概述

PooPal（"便便伙伴"）是一款个人生活习惯记录工具与趣味养成应用。我们非常重视您的隐私，本政策说明我们如何处理您的数据。

保存在设备上的数据

  • 排便日期与时间
  • 可选的 Bristol 形态、颜色和备注
  • 应用设置偏好（主题、通知时间等）

记录保存在设备本地，不上传至我们的服务器。本应用不访问 HealthKit，也不提供记录的 iCloud 同步。系统级备份由您的设备与系统设置管理。

购买与权益

购买与恢复购买由 Apple 处理。PooPal 在设备上验证 Apple 签名的交易信息，用于解锁 Pro 和识别原付费版购买权益；验证时不会发送您的排便记录。Apple 服务受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

第三方服务

本应用没有广告 SDK、第三方分析工具或追踪器。应用会联系 Apple 检查版本更新及处理购买。仅当您主动导出或分享时才会分享记录；您选择的接收应用或服务可能有其自身隐私政策。若您通过邮箱联系我们，我们会收到您主动发送的内容，用于处理您的支持请求。

数据存储与删除

所有记录数据存储在您的设备本地。您可以随时：

  • 在历史记录中左滑删除单条记录
  • 删除应用以清除全部数据

我们不保留您的记录副本。系统备份、已导出的文件和已分享的副本不会因删除应用内记录而自动删除，请在相应系统、文件或接收服务中管理。

儿童隐私

本应用不针对 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

健康免责声明

本应用仅为个人习惯记录工具，不构成任何形式的医疗诊断、健康评估或治疗建议。应用内展示的数据趋势和统计信息仅供参考。如有健康问题，请咨询专业医生。

隐私政策变更

本政策更新时会在此处展示，并调整最后更新日期。

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

PooPal is a personal habit tracking tool and fun virtual pet app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Bowel movement dates and times
  • Optional Bristol type, color and notes
  • App preferences (theme, notification settings, etc.)

Records are stored locally and are not uploaded to our servers. This app does not access HealthKit or provide iCloud record synchronization. System backups are controlled by your device and system settings.

Purchases & Access

Apple handles purchases and restoration. PooPal verifies Apple's signed transactions on your device to unlock Pro and recognize original paid-app purchases. Your bowel records are not sent with these checks. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

No advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or trackers are included. The app contacts Apple to check for updates and to manage purchases. Records are shared only when you choose to export or share them; the destination you select may have its own privacy policy. If you contact us by email, we receive the content you choose to send to handle your support request.

Data Storage & Deletion

All records are stored locally on your device. You can at any time:

  • Swipe to delete individual records in the history view
  • Delete the app to remove all data

We do not retain copies of your records. Deleting app records does not automatically delete system backups, exported files or shared copies. Manage those in the relevant system, file location or recipient service.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health Disclaimer

This app is a personal habit tracking tool only and does not constitute any form of medical diagnosis, health assessment, or treatment advice. Data trends and statistics displayed in the app are for reference only. Please consult a healthcare professional for any health concerns.
